## Why

Every later estimate (capture hours, OCR hours, storage, review time) rests on how many pages there are and what
kind. The series list is the skeleton of the archive's arrangement and of the club's visibility choices. The pilot
sample has to exist before D06 can run.

## Inputs

- The shoebox.
- A notebook or the survey sheet below; sticky notes or acid-free paper slips to mark groups (no new staples,
  paper clips or rubber bands on the records).

## Outputs (kept privately, with the records or in the author's notes; never in git)

1. **Count**: pages per group, to ±10%. Count a ¼-inch stack, measure the rest by thickness, and count bound
   items' pages from their last page number.
2. **Rough groups → draft series list**: e.g. yearbooks and programs; newsletters; flyers and event notices;
   minutes and handwritten records; membership lists and directories; photographs; newspaper clippings;
   correspondence; other. Use the club's own names for things if it has them.
3. **Per group**: date range (by decade is fine), material (typeset, typewritten, mimeograph or ditto,
   photocopy, handwritten, newsprint, photo print), condition (brittle, folded, faded, glossy, bound,
   oversized), and anything sensitive (member addresses or phone numbers, finances, private letters).
4. **Pilot sample**: about **50 pages** in a separate folder, chosen to cover every decade and every material,
   with extra of the hard cases: mimeograph, old typefaces, clippings, handwriting, photos with captions, a
   bound yearbook spread, a glossy or curled page. Note the group each came from so they go back in place.
5. **Physical notes**: anything too fragile to handle, anything that isn't paper (badges, ribbons, objects),
   anything oversized (larger than Letter/Legal).

## Steps

1. Lay out the box in its current order; photograph the order (top view) so it can be restored. That photo is
   private.
2. Sort into groups without changing the internal order of any folder or bundle (original order matters to an
   archive).
3. Count and fill in the survey sheet.
4. Pull the pilot sample with a marker slip left in each place a page was taken from.
5. Send the author the survey sheet (numbers and group names only).

## Acceptance criteria

- Total page count and per-group counts recorded; the total is within the expected 1,000–2,500 or the epic's
  estimates are redone (more than 3,000 pages adds capture sessions in D12 and moves R4 up).
- A draft series list with at least a name and date range per series.
- Pilot folder of 45–60 pages covering every decade and every material found.
- The original order is recorded and recoverable.

## Test plan

The author reads the sheet back with the archivist and spot-checks two groups' counts. D06 checks the sample
covers the materials listed.

## Privacy and rights

- [ ] The survey sheet lists groups and counts, not names, addresses or contents.
- [ ] Sensitive groups are flagged so D11's policies and D18's choices can cover them.
- [ ] Nothing leaves the archivist's custody except by agreement (Q2).

## Touches

Nothing in the repo. Its numbers feed D06, D10 (storage), D12 (sessions) and the epic's estimates.

## Done means

The author has the survey sheet, the draft series list and confirmation the pilot folder exists, by Wed Nov 25.
